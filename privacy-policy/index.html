Information Collection and Use

The Application may collect information when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (for example, IP address)
  • The pages or screens of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on those pages or screens
  • The time spent using the Application
  • The operating system and device information you use on your mobile device
  • Gameplay activity such as game interactions, scores, and leaderboard-related activity when optional game services are used

The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device.

The Application does not require users to provide personally identifiable information directly to the Service Provider.

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.

Aggregated or non-personally identifiable data may be transmitted to external services to help the Service Provider improve the Application. Third-party services used for advertising, diagnostics, performance, and optional game services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

The Application may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob Privacy Policy

The Application uses third-party advertising services to display ads, including Google AdMob and Unity Ads.

Google AdMob may collect and use data such as device identifiers, usage data, advertising data, and diagnostic information to provide personalized and non-personalized ads, measure ad performance, and help prevent fraud.

Unity Ads may collect and process data including device identifiers, IP address, usage data, advertising data, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention purposes. For more information, please visit: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Game Services and Leaderboards

The Application may use Apple Game Center and Google Play Games Services for optional leaderboard features. If you choose to use these features, Apple or Google may process information such as your player profile, player identifier, scores, leaderboard activity, and related gameplay information according to their own privacy policies.

Using game services is optional. The core gameplay remains available without using leaderboard features.

Tracking

On iOS, the Application may request permission to track users across apps and websites using Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework.

This permission allows advertising partners, such as Google AdMob and Unity Ads, to provide more relevant ads and improve ad performance.

If you choose not to allow tracking, you will still see ads, but they may be less relevant.

Data Collection

The Service Provider does not directly collect personal account data through its own account system. However, third-party services such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Google Play Games Services, and Apple Game Center may collect certain information automatically as described in their own privacy policies.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by uninstalling the Application. You may also manage advertising and tracking preferences through your device settings where available.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider does not require users to create an account and does not directly store personal user account data on its own servers. Some third-party services used by the Application may retain data according to their own privacy policies.

For privacy questions or data-related requests, contact: support@tytrgames.com

Children

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If the Service Provider becomes aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information, reasonable steps will be taken to delete such information where applicable.

Security

The Service Provider takes reasonable measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

This Privacy Policy is effective as of 2026-05-31.

Your Consent
